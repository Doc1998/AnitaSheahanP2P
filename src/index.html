About Path to Peace

Therapy that meets you where you are

Path to Peace was founded on a simple belief: that quality, compassionate therapy should be accessible to everyone in Ireland — not just those who can travel to a clinic or fit into a 9–5 schedule.

With fully online sessions, Anita Sheehan brings warm, integrative therapy directly to you — whether you're in Dublin, Donegal, or anywhere in between. There's no commute, no waiting room anxiety, and no barrier to getting the support you deserve.

Areas of Support

How I can help you

Whether you're navigating a difficult period or looking to grow, therapy provides a confidential, non-judgemental space to do that work.

Mental Health & Chronic Health Conditions

A chronic illness diagnosis affects far more than your physical health. Anita provides compassionate support for the emotional and psychological impact of living with long-term health conditions — including grief, identity shifts, and health anxiety.

Anxiety

Anxiety can feel overwhelming and all-consuming. Through evidence-based approaches, Anita helps you understand your anxiety, develop practical tools to manage it, and gradually reclaim the life you want to be living.

Depression

Depression can make even the simplest tasks feel impossible. Therapy offers a space to explore what's underneath, address unhelpful thought patterns, and rebuild a sense of hope and purpose — one session at a time.

Personal Development

Therapy isn't only for crisis — it's also a powerful tool for growth. If you're seeking greater self-awareness, stronger relationships, more confidence, or a clearer sense of direction, Anita can support that journey too.

Meet Your Therapist

Hi, I'm Anita Sheehan

I'm a qualified counsellor and psychotherapist working with individuals across Ireland through online therapy. I founded Path to Peace because I believe that everyone — regardless of where they live or what they're going through — deserves compassionate, professional support.

My work draws on person-centred therapy, cognitive behavioural approaches, and mindfulness-based techniques. I'm particularly passionate about supporting people navigating the psychological side of chronic illness, anxiety, depression, and those looking to understand themselves more deeply through personal development work.

Above all, I aim to provide a warm, non-judgemental space where you can feel truly heard.
