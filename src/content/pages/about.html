My Story

A therapist who truly listens

My name is Anita Sheehan and I'm a qualified counsellor and psychotherapist, accredited with the Irish Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (IACP). I founded Path to Peace because I believe that access to compassionate, professional therapy should never be limited by geography or circumstance.

I work with individuals from all walks of life, all across Ireland, through fully online sessions. Whether you're in a rural county with limited local services, a busy professional who can't easily leave work, or simply someone who feels more comfortable opening up from the privacy of home — online therapy removes the barrier and puts quality support within reach.

"My goal is simple: to provide a warm, non-judgemental space where you feel truly heard — and to walk alongside you as you find your own path to peace."

— Anita Sheehan

My work draws on a range of evidence-based approaches, including person-centred therapy, cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), and mindfulness-based techniques. Rather than applying a one-size-fits-all model, I tailor my approach to each individual — meeting you where you are, at a pace that feels right for you.

I have a particular interest in supporting people who are navigating the psychological and emotional side of living with chronic health conditions. A diagnosis can turn your world upside down — affecting your identity, your relationships, and your sense of the future. I understand that experience, and I'm here to help you process it.

Alongside that, I work with clients experiencing anxiety, depression, and those using therapy as a tool for personal development and deeper self-understanding. Whatever brings you here, you don't have to face it alone.

How I Work

My therapeutic approach

I draw on a range of evidence-based modalities, always tailored to the individual. No two people are the same — and your therapy shouldn't be either.

Person-Centred Therapy

At the heart of everything I do. This approach treats you as the expert of your own life, offering unconditional positive regard, empathy, and genuine warmth. The therapeutic relationship itself is the foundation for change.

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)

A structured, practical approach that helps you identify and shift unhelpful thought patterns and behaviours. Particularly effective for anxiety and depression, CBT gives you tools you can use long after therapy ends.

Mindfulness-Based Approaches

Drawing on mindfulness and acceptance-based techniques, I help clients develop a kinder, more present relationship with their thoughts and feelings — reducing reactivity and building emotional resilience over time.
